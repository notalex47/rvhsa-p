Modifications: source OBJ geometry was converted to compact indexed binary
buffers, coordinates were rotated to Y-up and converted from mm to metres,
and positions were rounded to 0.001 mm. Vertex normals are recalculated at
runtime. Anatomical landmarks are selected with surface masks. The coccyx and
pubic symphysis supplements and the orbital/nasal perimeter markers are
original simplified additions. These adapted model data and supplements are
redistributed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.

Version 1.3.1: the coccyx supplement was reshaped and positioned using an anchor
derived from the inferior sacral mesh, with closed ends and a small root overlap.

Version 1.3.2: six simplified original costal-cartilage supplements were added
for rib pairs 8–10. Their anchors are derived from the anterior rib tips and
the seventh costal cartilages, with each lower cartilage joining the one above.
These are teaching approximations, not additional BodyParts3D scan meshes,
and are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan license.

Three.js and OrbitControls are from the official Three.js r170 distribution:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r170
The import path in OrbitControls.js was changed to the bundled local Three.js.
Their MIT license is in vendor/LICENSE-three.txt.

Quiz blurbs are original short explanations of anatomical facts. Reference
links to OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e are provided in the quiz's About
section. No textbook images, diagrams, or verbatim passages were copied.
