About the model & anatomy references

Bone surfaces come from BodyParts3D 4.0. Colored surface regions identify the ilium, ischium, pubis, acromion, transverse processes, and femoral condyles. Orange perimeter markers identify the eye sockets and anterior nasal aperture. The coccyx, pubic symphysis, and lower costal cartilages for ribs 8–10 are simplified original supplements. These region boundaries and supplements are teaching approximations. False ribs include pairs 8–12; floating ribs (11–12) form a subset.
