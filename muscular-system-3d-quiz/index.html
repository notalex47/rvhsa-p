About the model & anatomy references

This model combines BodyParts3D muscle surfaces with a skeletal reference. Each target includes every modeled head or portion of the named muscle, on both sides. Six muscles unavailable in this dataset use original simplified teaching models: temporalis, orbicularis oculi, pars peripheralis of orbicularis oris, occipitofrontalis, rectus abdominis, and latissimus dorsi. Their shapes and boundaries are approximations. Occipitofrontalis includes both frontal and occipital bellies and its connecting epicranial aponeurosis; the aponeurosis is shown as a thin sheet. Pars peripheralis is highlighted as a complete peripheral band, excluding the marginal lip region. The view omits skin and many deeper structures.
