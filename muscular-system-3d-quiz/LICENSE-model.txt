Source component IDs and English names are preserved in muscles.json.

Modifications: converted source OBJ meshes to compact indexed binary buffers;
rotated Z-up coordinates to Y-up, converted millimeters to meters, and rounded
positions to 0.001 mm. Runtime recalculates vertex normals. No target source
muscles are cropped: all included heads/portions and both sides are highlighted.

14 original simplified supplement components represent six targets absent
from the selected source dataset: temporalis, orbicularis oculi, pars peripheralis
of orbicularis oris, occipitofrontalis (frontal and occipital bellies plus epicranial
aponeurosis), rectus abdominis, and latissimus dorsi. Supplements are teaching
approximations, not scanned anatomy; pars peripheralis is represented as a
peripheral band excluding the marginal lip region. Scalp surfaces are fitted
to the source skull. Source definitions are in supplements.mjs; baked data in
muscles.json/muscles.bin is used at runtime. All adapted geometry and original
supplements are redistributed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.

The shared skeleton is loaded from ../skeletal-system-quiz/skeleton.json and
skeleton.bin. Its attribution and modifications are described in that folder's
LICENSE-model.txt. Its source bone geometry is retained unchanged here.
