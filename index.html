Skeletal System: Bundled False Rib Repair
Version 1.3.5 · October 4, 2026
  • Bundled all six lower costal-cartilage sections directly into the model data, connecting rib pairs 8–10 on both sides.
  • Kept the connections visible during exploration and false-rib questions; floating ribs 11–12 retain free anterior ends.
  • Updated both model-data URLs and quiz modules together so the repair loads as one version.
  • Added regression checks for bundled geometry, attachment overlap, and highlight visibility.
  • Preserved the refreshed website design, tailbone repair, free-response names, saved progress, and multiple-choice randomization.
